## Delivery notes

- Read it like a builder showing a friend, not like a product narrator. Slight pauses after the commas.
- Keep "zero point zero two five" spoken out — it matches the on-screen size and avoids a robotic number read.
- Never say "as an AI" or "I'm delighted" — the point is a human demo.
