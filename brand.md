# Brand

## Name and what we do
Position Glance — a hands-free Hyperliquid testnet position app for Even G2 smart glasses. One position, one spoken intent, one confirmed reduce-only order.

## Audience
Crypto traders on the move who watch a position during the day and want a glance instead of pulling out a phone; plus judges and developers reviewing the open-source build.

## What we promote (with the real link)
The open-source repository: https://github.com/aishwaryanevrekar/position-glance

## The feeling
Premium, technical, calm, bold — quiet confidence, no hype.

## Assets
Colours (typed, not sampled): near-black `#0B0E0C`, surface `#121812`, signal green `#8DFF9B`, dim green `#3D7F4B`, warm white `#E5E5E5`, amber accent `#FFB27A`.
Fonts: Menlo / SFMono-Regular / system monospace. No logo file — the wordmark is set in type, not redrawn.

## Never
No invented numbers, fake UI, or financial advice. Every name, date and number on screen comes from a source we approved.
